About Us

Daily Tez Khabar — ہر خبر حقیقت

Daily Tez Khabar (روزنامہ تیز خبر) is a daily newspaper published from Lahore, Pakistan. Our promise is printed on our masthead: every news item is the truth. We bring readers the day's news quickly, and we check it before it goes to print.

This website carries each day's edition online, so readers in Lahore, across Pakistan and abroad can read Daily Tez Khabar wherever they are.

  • Truth firstWe report facts and stand behind what we print.
  • Fast and fairQuick to the news, fair to every side of a story.
  • Rooted in LahoreWritten for Pakistani readers, at home and abroad.
